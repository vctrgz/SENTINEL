# Skill: execution_summarization

## Description
Summarize execution results into concise information.

---

## Capabilities
- Aggregate multiple command results
- Highlight key outcomes

---

## How to Think
- Focus on outcome, not logs
- Keep summary minimal

---

## Rules
- DO NOT include raw logs
- DO NOT add irrelevant details

---

## When to Use
- Before sending result to orchestrator

---

## Output Expectations
Plain text summary

---

## Examples

### Output
"Los comandos se ejecutaron correctamente y los archivos fueron listados."
